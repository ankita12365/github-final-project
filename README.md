# Simple Interest Calculator

A script designed to calculate simple interest based on the principal amount, annual rate of interest, and time period in years.

## Formula
$$\text{Simple Interest} = \frac{\text{Principal} \times \text{Rate} \times \text{Time}}{100}$$

## Inputs
- **Principal (P):** The initial amount of money invested or borrowed.
- **Rate (R):** The annual interest rate (in percentage).
- **Time (T):** The time period the money is invested or borrowed for (in years).

## Output
- **Simple Interest (SI):** Total interest accrued over the given time period.
